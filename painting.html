Painting

I have been painting since teenage age. Initially I had a great interest in portraits and surrealistic images. During my study of Art Therapy between 2007-2011, where 1 year was dedicated to developing your own artistic language, I discovered the colour field and the dimension of space on canvas. This fascination remains until today.

The paintings presented here are abstract and expressive. I work with spatulas, rollers, sponges and hands, layering paint and removing parts of it again, until the feeling of saturation and coherence in the picture sets in. The end of a process is spontaneous, then I intuitively feel „the painting is finished (for now)“.

Over the last couple of years I made a series of paintings I call “Space-scapes”. They are transmitting an atmosphere through light, colour and textures, creating fields layered horizontally across the canvas. These compositions may have a resemblance with landscapes stripped of all objects in space, hence “Space-scapes”, concentrating on the essential quality of a particular natural environment. Included within this exploration are considerations around saturation/materiality of space and it’s transcending and ethereal nature.

Some of my paintings are still available for sale. Please contact me in case of interest.
