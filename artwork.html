Artwork

“Dealing with the inherent beauty of materials and their unfailing reliability can involve us in a deeply healing process.”

Joan M. Erikson

I always had an urge to create something with my hands. Something that pleases the eye and can be used practically. The love of beauty constantly inspires me to arrange and modulate my surroundings and to contemplate the inherent perfection of life.

I use art as a way of processing life experiences, thoughts and emotions. A way of asking questions and formulating understanding. And I never cease to attempt hinting towards the majesty of existence.

Please enjoy your stroll around and don´t hesitate to contact me, if you are interested to purchase a piece or would like to be informed, when I have an exhibition.
